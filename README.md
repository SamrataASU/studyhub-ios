# StudyHub for iPhone

Turns your Canvas calendar feed into a clean deadline tracker: countdowns, a Monday–Sunday progress ring,
reminders before each deadline, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a focus timer, and daily finance news from Google News.

Everything stays on your phone. The app only downloads your Canvas calendar (from the link you paste) and public headlines.

## Run it
Open `StudyHub.xcodeproj` in Xcode, choose your iPhone, set **Signing & Capabilities → Team** for the
`StudyHub` and `StudyHubWidgetExtension` targets, and press Run.

Every change pushed here is test-built automatically (see the **Actions** tab).

The Android version lives in `studyhub-android`.
